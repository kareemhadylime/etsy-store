How to use these prompts

Eight decisions every practicing Muslim hits across a Hijri year. Eight prompts you can copy, paste, and adapt — designed for the free tier of your favourite AI assistant.

Each prompt is built to pair with a specific tab in your Zakat Calculator. You'll see the tab name on each page — paste, fill in placeholders, send.

How it works
  1. Open the prompt page you need (Setup Wizard, Crypto/DeFi, etc.).
  2. Open the matching tab in your spreadsheet.
  3. Copy the prompt into your favourite AI assistant.
  4. Replace the [PLACEHOLDERS] with your data.
  5. Read the worked example on the same page to see what good output looks like.
What you'll need
  • An account with any AI assistant (the free tier works)
  • Your Zakat Calculator open in another window
  • Your Madhhab selected on ⚙️ Madhhab Settings (drives ruling logic everywhere)
  • 15 minutes the first time; ~5 minutes once familiar
What this PDF will and won't do

It WILL: Calculate Zakat amounts. Walk through scholar comparisons. Generate a distribution plan across the 8 eligible categories. Draft an annual report. Cite mainstream rulings from NZF UK, AAOIFI Standard 21, Islamic Relief, and AMP India in every response.

It WON'T: Issue fatwas. Replace consultation with a qualified mufti. Submit Zakat for you. Choose your Madhhab. Pay your charity on your behalf.

Scholar disclaimer

Calculations and AI guidance are based on mainstream scholarly positions. Not a substitute for consultation with a qualified mufti for your specific situation. Mainstream rulings can differ across Madhhabs; this PDF surfaces those differences honestly rather than presenting one ruling as universal.

Privacy

Your wealth details, family financial information, and Madhhab selection never enter any AI tool unless you paste them. Use the spreadsheet's labels ("Cash Account A," "Gulf EOSB") instead of bank account numbers or institution names. Everything happens in your own AI account.

Worked Example — Persona A (Hanafi · ~$20K wealth)
Output (excerpt)

NZF UK §2.1Gold Nisab: 87.48g × ~$72/g ≈ $6,300 USD threshold. Prophet Muhammad ﷺ instructed 20 dinars (≈ 87.48g). Universal across Madhhabs as the gold-equivalent threshold.

AMP India §1.3Silver Nisab: 612.36g × ~$0.95/g ≈ $582 USD threshold. 200 dirhams (≈ 612.36g). Same hadith origin; Hanafi school adopts silver as operational reference.

The contemporary debate: At revelation, gold-Nisab ≈ silver-Nisab in purchasing power. Today, silver / gold ratio ~85:1 vs historical 16:1 → silver-Nisab today is ~10× lower in dollar terms.

NZF UK §2.3"Silver-Nisab ensures more Muslims contribute to communal welfare and more recipients benefit. Aligns with the original spirit of Zakat as wealth-redistribution."

YOUR PRACTICAL DIFFERENCE: At ~$20K, you're WELL ABOVE both thresholds. The choice is symbolic/principled, not threshold-determinative. Method matters at $2K-$6K cohort.

RECOMMENDED: Silver-Nisab. Aligns with Hanafi tradition + NZF UK contemporary recommendation. At your level both make you obligated; aligning costs you nothing AND keeps you consistent across years.

TRADE-OFF: Silver spot is more volatile than gold. Some years silver Nisab is $450; other years $620. Re-verify each year.

CONSISTENCY: Pick silver this year. Stick with it. Don't toggle year-to-year based on which produces a lower obligation — that's optimizer-thinking, not Zakat practice.

Which AI should I use?

All eight prompts work in free tiers. Differences come down to handling of citations + multi-asset complexity.

Conversational, follow-up editing

Best for: Setup Wizard, Distribution Planner, Qada Recovery Coach, Annual Report PDF. Most AI assistants have a conversational tone, write narrative summaries + emotional framings cleanly, and are fluent at iterating tone and softening difficult content (Qada is sensitive).

Limit: Free tiers cap how much you can paste per message (~3,000–4,000 words). Longer prompts with many citations + complex worked examples may stretch context — batch large portfolios across multiple turns.

Longer structured input

Best for: Crypto/DeFi Zakat, Nisab Method Picker, Stocks Method Picker, Pension Analyser. Some assistants are better at structured per-asset analysis, multi-source citation, and Madhhab-comparison tables, and handle longer scholarly citation traces in one paste.

Limit: Free tiers often cap conversations per day. If you hit it, switch to another AI assistant for the next prompt.

A paid AI plan

Worth it if you're running complex multi-asset analysis annually + tracking missed years across multiple Hijri cycles. For straightforward annual calculation (simple cash + gold + silver), free tier is fully sufficient.

Universal tips
  1. Citations are starting points, not final fatwas. The AI cites mainstream rulings from NZF UK / AAOIFI / Islamic Relief / AMP India. For specific edge cases — your Sukuk's exact structure, your pension's accessibility specifics, contested DeFi treatment — verify with a qualified mufti before relying on the AI's interpretation.
  2. Never paste bank account numbers, institution names, family names, or SSN. Use spreadsheet labels ("Cash Account A," "Gulf EOSB"). The AI doesn't need real identifiers to do the math.
  3. Scholarly disagreement is normal. When the AI presents two scholarly positions on a contested point (gold vs silver Nisab, crypto staking, etc.), pick the one that aligns with your Madhhab + your conscience. Stick with the choice across years.
  4. Cross-Madhhab comparison is a learning tool, not a shopping cart. The AI explains how different Madhhabs treat the same situation. Use this to understand your tradition better — not to "shop" for the most favorable ruling.
  5. The Scholar Disclaimer on every tab is your ongoing reminder. The AI is a thinking partner. A qualified mufti is your scholar. Use both.
