Setup Guide
v1.0
3 steps
1
Open the link in your purchase email
The email from Lime Premium Studios contains a "Open & duplicate" button. Click it. The Notion template opens in your browser. If you're not logged in to Notion, you'll be prompted — free Notion accounts work just fine.
If you don't have a Notion account, sign up free at notion.so first, then come back and click the link.
2
Click "Duplicate" at the top-right of the page
Notion shows a Duplicate button in the top-right corner of the page, just under your account menu. Click it.
3
Pick your workspace
If you have more than one Notion workspace, Notion will ask which one to copy into. Pick the personal workspace where you want your budget to live. The template lands in your sidebar instantly.

Page 3 of 5 · First-day actions
Your first 5 minutes inside.
The seed data shows what the template can do. Replace it with your real numbers, one short step at a time.
Setup Guide
v1.0
5 actions · ~5 minutes
1
Open 💵 Income — add your most recent paycheck
Click the database row marked "Paycheck — Acme Corp" and either edit it to match your real income or click + New to add a fresh row. Keep at least one example row visible until you're comfortable with the structure.
2
Open 🎯 Budget by Category — adjust the 5 starter categories
Categories ship as Groceries / Transport / Dining / Subscriptions / Shopping with sensible monthly targets ($500 / $250 / $200 / $80 / $150). Rename them to match your life and reset the Target ($/mo) column. Add new categories with + New if you need them.
3
Open 💳 Expense — add the last 3 expenses you remember
Just type from memory — coffee, gas, groceries, an Amazon order. Tag each one with a category from your updated Budget list. The Home dashboard's KPI row updates as you go.
4
Open 🔁 Recurring Bills — log your rent + 2 biggest bills
Rent / mortgage, internet, phone — whatever auto-charges every month. Set the Due Day, Frequency, and Last Paid date. The Next Due column computes itself from there, and the Status pill turns yellow when something's coming up in 7 days.
5
Come back to 🏠 Home — see your numbers
The 3-callout KPI row at the top of Home now reflects you, not the seed family. Spent / Budget / Remaining update live as you add to Expense. Bookmark this page in Notion's sidebar — it's the daily landing surface.

Common edits
Rename a category 2 minutes
Open 🎯 Budget by Category → click the category name → type the new name. The relation in 💳 Expense updates automatically — no manual fixup needed. Renaming "Groceries" to "Food" keeps all linked expenses tied.
Add a new category 1 minute
In 🎯 Budget by Category click + New → fill in Name, Icon (an emoji), Color (Blue / Green / Yellow / Red only — per the design system), and the monthly target. The Home dashboard's gallery view picks it up on next refresh.
Change the currency 3 minutes
Each numeric column with $ formatting is configured as Notion's Dollar number type. Click any Amount column header → Edit property → Number format → pick your currency (Notion supports EUR, GBP, JPY, etc.). Repeat in Income · Expense · Budget · Recurring · Subscriptions.
Archive instead of delete 1 minute
For old months you want out of view but not gone, add an "Archived" checkbox property to any database and filter the default views to Archived ≠ true. Deleting a row also works — Notion keeps it in Trash for 30 days.

Notion AI works on any of these databases out of the box — try "/ AI" inside any cell. The dedicated AI Advisor page with 30+ pre-written prompts ships in the AI Edition tier once Essentials sells through. Watch for an email upgrade offer.

Common issues
Q. The KPI row on Home shows zeros.
The 3 KPI cards pull from the inline 📊 Dashboard Stats database. Notion rollups need at least one row in Expense and one in Budget by Category to compute — they're seeded for you, so if everything reads zero, you probably deleted the seed rows. Add one expense + one budget category to restore.
Q. The Sync indicator spins forever.
Notion occasionally lags on duplicate of large workspaces. Hard-refresh the page (Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + R). If formulas still show —, give Notion 2-3 minutes to finish the duplicate operation in the background.
Q. Mobile looks different.
Notion's mobile apps collapse 3-column callout rows into a single stack. The KPI row, dashboard, and linked views all still work — they just stack vertically. The template was designed dashboard-first so mobile scrolling stays clean.
Q. I'm on Notion's free tier.
Every feature in this template works on Notion Free: databases, rollups, formulas, calendar views, linked databases, page covers. Notion's free-tier limit (1,000 blocks) is more than enough for personal budgeting — most buyers stay under 200 blocks for years.
Q. I want to share with my partner.
Click Share at the top-right of any page → invite by email. Notion's free tier supports a few guest collaborators per workspace. For a fully shared household budget you'll want a Notion Plus workspace ($10/mo) — but that's Notion's pricing, not ours.
Q. Notion changed something and my template broke.
Email support@limepremiumstudios.com — we ship free updates when Notion makes breaking changes. Include a screenshot of the breakage and we'll get you a patched duplicate link within a few days.
