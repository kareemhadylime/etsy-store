Page 2 · The Setup Order
Set them up in this order.
Six spreadsheets, six dependencies. Setting them up out of order means redoing categories, lists, and balances twice.
Time
~60 min
The principle. Budget Tracker is the foundation — it defines your income, fixed expenses, and saving capacity. Sinking Funds and Debt Payoff borrow Budget's categories. Net Worth pulls balances from Debt Payoff and from your bank/account list. Small Business is independent but its net profit feeds back into Budget. Wedding sits at the end — it borrows from Sinking Funds (the "Wedding" sub-fund) and pushes its monthly burn back into Budget once the planning starts.
1
📊 Budget Tracker
Foundation. Defines income, expenses, savings capacity. Everything borrows from here.
2
🪣 Sinking Funds
Needs Budget's available-to-sink number. Wedding will become a sub-fund here.
3
💰 Net Worth Tracker
Pulls liability balances from Debt Payoff, asset balances from accounts. Needs account list ready.
4
📉 Debt Payoff
Use the same debt list from Net Worth. Calculations need APRs already entered there.
5
🏢 Small Business
Mostly standalone. Monthly net profit feeds back into Budget Income as "Side Business" line.
6
💍 Wedding
Borrows Wedding sub-fund from Sinking Funds. Its monthly burn flows back into Budget once vendor deposits start.
Each product has an Input tab and an Output Dashboard. Input tabs are where you enter data. Output Dashboards visualize what you entered. Edit Input tabs only — Dashboards update automatically.

Page 10 · Troubleshooting + Support
When something doesn't add up.
Six issues we see most often, with the one-line fix for each. If yours isn't here, email us.
Need help
24-hr reply
My Net Worth and Debt Payoff debt totals don't match.
First Saturday of each month: open both, copy the current balance from Debt Payoff into Net Worth Liabilities. 30 seconds, done.
Wedding fund balance in Sinking Funds doesn't match Wedding spreadsheet.
Wedding reads Sinking Funds Wedding fund row on setup, not continuously. After any contribution to the Wedding sub-fund, also update the Wedding spreadsheet's "Saved to date" field on the Wedding Setup tab.
My Budget says I have $500/mo to save but Sinking Funds shows the funds are behind.
Sinking Funds calculates required-monthly from target dates. If $500 covers your funds but math wants $720 to hit all targets, that's a real shortfall. Use the Priority Matrix to decide which fund slips its target date.
Small Business profit shows up but Budget income doesn't reflect it.
Small Business doesn't auto-push to Budget. End of each month, open the Small Business P&L, note net profit, then open Budget Income tab and add a "Side Business" line for that amount.
I changed a category name in Budget — now Sinking Funds shows the old name.
Sinking Funds borrowed Budget's category list at setup. They don't auto-sync. Rename in both places when you rename in one.
Excel formulas show #VALUE! or #REF! errors when I open in Sheets.
Use File → Import → Replace spreadsheet instead of opening the .xlsx directly. Google's importer handles formula translation. If a specific cell still errors, email us with the cell address — we ship a patch.
