LAST 30 DAYS OF EXPENSE DATA (paste from Expense Tracker, sorted by date): [PASTE TABLE: Date · Category · Vendor · Amount] TARGET CUT: $[amount]/month HOUSEHOLD CONTEXT: income $[amount]/month, region [city], household size [number] Find me three things: 1. THREE RANKED SPENDING CUTS with exact dollar amounts, ordered by least-painful first. ONE NON-OBVIOUS PATTERN you noticed — something that looks fine on the surface but is actually a leak (subscription that auto-renewed at a higher rate, vendor I'm being charged at twice, "category creep" where one category gradually expanded). 3. ONE CATEGORY I'M UNDER-SPENDING ON that might be hurting me long-term (no savings, no emergency fund, no professional development). Stay specific. Cite vendors + dollar amounts from my data.
