Three Things People Wish They'd Known on Day One
01
Run it weekly, not daily.
10 minutes every Sunday beats 2 minutes every day. Budgets work when you see the pattern, not the transaction.
02
Tag tax-deductible as you go.
Toggle the Tax-Deductible flag in Expense Tracker. Year-end you'll thank yourself when CPA prep takes 15 minutes instead of 4 hours.
03
Don't optimize what doesn't matter.
Housing is 40% of most budgets and almost never the lever. Look at the next-biggest 3 — that's where the dollars actually move.
