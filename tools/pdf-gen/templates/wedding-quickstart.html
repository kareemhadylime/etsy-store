First 30 days — the suggested order
  1. Day 1. Setup Wizard → Budget Dashboard. Read the numbers. Don't panic; you have 12+ months.
  2. Week 1. Guest List rough draft (over-include — easier to cut than add).
  3. Week 2. Tour 3 venues + sign the one you love. The venue anchors the date and the budget.
  4. Week 3. Book photographer + caterer (longest-lead vendors).
  5. Week 4. If AI Edition: run the Guest List Optimizer prompt with a tighter target — refine to the real number.

Common Wedding Events
Brief descriptions and typical budget share for each event type.
Tab
Multi-Event
MENA ceremonies
Event Description Typical guests Budget share
Katb-Kitab Arabic contract-signing ceremony. Sheikh or officiant presides. Mahr agreed between families. Intimate, often at home or a private room. Light catering, moderate decor, photographer. 30–80 5–10% of total
Henna / Mehndi Night Pre-wedding henna celebration, typically the evening before the wedding. Festive dress (not bridal white). Henna artist is the longest-lead booking — secure 3–4 months out. DJ or live percussion common. 30–150 8–12% of total
South Asian ceremonies
Event Description Typical guests Budget share
Sangeet Musical evening 1–2 nights before the wedding. Family and friends perform choreographed dances. Requires a stage or open floor, sound system, and strong photography / videography. Choreography classes 2–3 months prior are a common overlooked cost. 80–200+ 10–15% of total
Mehndi Henna ceremony for the bride and female guests, often held separately from Sangeet or combined. Henna artist skill level varies widely — review portfolio. Often coincides with bridal shower activities. 20–80 4–8% of total
Western / universal events
Event Description Typical guests Budget share
Rehearsal Dinner Evening before the wedding for wedding party and close family. Often hosted by groom's family per tradition, though this varies. Restaurant private dining room is the most common venue. Photography is optional — most couples skip it. 20–50 3–6% of total
Engagement Party Celebration of the engagement announcement, often hosted by one set of parents. Timing varies widely — some hold it the week of the engagement, others 6 months before the wedding. Budget as a separate event. 30–120 5–10% of total
Budgeting across multiple events
Set a total envelope first. Allocate sub-budgets per event in the Multi-Event Planner tab. The reception commands the largest share (50–70% typical). Pre-wedding events together rarely exceed 25–30% in a well-managed plan. The Multi-Event Planner tab tracks each event independently so a reception overage does not silently absorb headroom from your Henna or Sangeet budget.
