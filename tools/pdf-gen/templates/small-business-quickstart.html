First-hour setup map
Tab Cells What to enter
💵 Revenue Tracker C12:I111 One row per revenue transaction. Date · Client · Product/Service · Amount · Status (Received/Pending/Refunded) · Stream (Direct/Etsy/Wholesale) · Payment method. Drives P&L, Cash Flow, Profitability, and every Dashboard KPI.
💸 Expense Tracker C12:J161 One row per expense. Date · Category · Vendor · Amount · Tax-deductible flag (✅) · 📎 receipt-attached marker · Schedule C category · Notes. Tax flag + Schedule C category drive the Tax Prep Summary auto-roll.
🧾 Invoice Tracker B11:J60 Invoice # · Client · Issue date · Due date · Amount · Paid date · Status (Draft/Sent/Paid/Overdue/Cancelled) · Notes. Days outstanding auto-calcs. DSO at top of tab.
📄 Invoice Templates B7:J28 Replace [YOUR LOGO] · [Your Business Name] · [Your Address] with your details. Duplicate the entire template sub-sheet for each new invoice. Print to PDF, send via email.
🏦 Balance Sheet (Pro) C13:G33 Monthly snapshot of Assets · Liabilities + Equity. Some lines auto-pull (cash, AR, inventory, retained earnings); others (prepaid, AP, owner contrib) are manual entry. Auto-balance verification row at bottom.
💰 Loan Amortization (Pro) B12:J16 Up to 5 loans. Loan name · Origin date · APR (as decimal — 7.5% = 0.075) · Original principal · Term in months. Monthly payment + outstanding balance + payoff date auto-calc.
👥 HR Records (Pro) B12:J21 ID · Name · Role · Start · Type (FT/PT/Contractor) · Pay rate · Tax code · Leave days · Bank reference. Use REFERENCE values for bank account ("Chase ••••4242"). Never paste full account numbers or SSNs.
💰 Payroll (Pro) B12:J21 Hours · Gross · Federal · State · FICA (auto 7.65%) · Other · Net (auto-calc). This kit CALCULATES; it doesn't FILE or REMIT. Use Gusto/Justworks for the deposit + filing piece.
🤖 AI Advisor (AI) B8:E47 8 prompt cards in 2×4 grid. Each card pairs with a tab + a page in the companion AI PDF. Copy prompt → paste into your favourite AI assistant → paste output back into the card's collection cell.
Anti-foot-gun reminders
⚠
APR as decimal
In 💰 Loan Amortization col D, 7.5% = 0.075 (not 7.5). The input validation will reject anything > 0.99. Same rule for Asset Depreciation methods.
⚠
COGS vs OpEx coding
In 💸 Expense Tracker col I, use "COGS" exactly (case-sensitive) for direct cost-of-goods. Anything else = OpEx. The P&L Gross Margin calc splits on this string.
⚠
Don't paste SSNs / EINs
When using the AI prompts (AI Edition), use the spreadsheet's labels ("Customer A," "Employee #7"). The AI doesn't need full identifiers to analyze your books.

Page 3 · First-30-Day Playbook
Your first month
Week-by-week. End at first month-close + handoff-ready Tax Prep Summary.
Pages
3 of 3
Week 1
Migrate + log
  • Make a copy → save to Drive → bookmark.
  • Replace seed rows in 💵 Revenue Tracker and 💸 Expense Tracker with the last 30 days from your bank statements. Use Schedule C categories on every expense.
  • Open 🏠 Dashboard. Verify the 6 KPI tiles populate. If MTD Revenue shows $0, you skipped the date column on Revenue rows.
  • Set up Invoice Templates (📄) with your logo placeholder, business address, and a sample line-item rate sheet.
Week 2
Receivables + recurring
  • Load 🧾 Invoice Tracker with every outstanding invoice. Set status pills accurately (Draft / Sent / Paid / Overdue).
  • (Pro+) Set up 📅 Recurring Invoice Schedule for monthly/quarterly retainers. Mark them Active and pick a next-generate date.
  • (Pro+) Pull your last 90 days of overdue invoices into ⏳ Receivables Aging by bucket. Anything > 60 days: send a polite follow-up email this week.
Week 3
Operations
  • (Pro+) Build out 📦 Inventory Tracker if you sell physical product. Set reorder points; the low-stock pill fires automatically.
  • (Pro+) Load 👥 HR Records + 💰 Payroll for the next pay period. Verify net pay against your last paycheck/Gusto run before relying on it.
  • (Pro+) Load 🏗️ Asset Depreciation. Use straight-line as the default; replace with MACRS/§179 figures from your last tax return.
  • (Pro+) Load 💰 Loan Amortization for every business loan. Monthly payment auto-calcs from APR + term.
Week 4
First month-close
  • 📊 P&L Statement: verify your gross margin % matches your gut. If COGS is materially off, check the Schedule C coding in 💸 Expense Tracker.
  • 💧 Cash Flow Statement: check the danger ribbon at the bottom. Two consecutive negative months = it fires red.
  • (Pro+) 📈 KPI Dashboard: read all 8 KPIs. Note your gross margin %, net margin %, runway. These become next month's baseline.
  • (Pro+) 🔮 Cash Flow Forecast: set your minimum buffer. Fill in expected inflows/outflows for the next 12 weeks. The status pills surface danger weeks immediately.
  • 🧮 Tax Prep Summary: should be 80% populated. Export as PDF; email to your accountant. Schedule a 30-min call.
  • (AI Edition) Run the Annual Business Review prompt on your first full month's data — it sets the targets you'll measure against for the rest of the year.
