Three Things People Wish They'd Known on Day One
01
Avalanche saves more. Snowball wins more.
Avalanche (highest APR first) saves $1,000–$4,000 on a typical $30k debt load. Snowball (smallest first) keeps you going emotionally. Math vs psychology — pick the one you'll finish.
02
Pay before statement close, not due date.
Credit bureaus report your CC balance on statement-close date. Pay it down BEFORE close → utilization on your credit report drops → score jumps. The Utilization Optimizer tab does the math for you.
03
One missed payment = $35 + score crash.
Late payments hit your FICO for 7 years. Set autopay for the MINIMUM on every debt; pay more manually. The Late-Fee Alert tab catches what autopay misses (annual fees, promo expiry, statement changes).

Page 2 · Setup Walkthrough

Filling the Debt List, field by field.

The single tab that drives every other number in the workbook. Get this right and the rest is automatic.
Time
~3 min
The 10 fields on each debt row
FieldFormatWhy it mattersWatch out for
Debt Name Free text Identifies the debt across every tab. Use a name you'd recognize on a statement. Avoid the literal card number — use the issuer + last 4 if needed.
Type Dropdown Drives the Strategy Comparison color coding + the Student Loan / Medical Debt special tabs. Pick from the list — typed-in values lose categorization.
Balance $#,##0.00 Current payoff amount as of today. Drives every total on the Dashboard. Don't enter the credit limit — that's the line, not the debt.
APR 0.22 = 22% The single most important number. Avalanche ranks debts by APR. DECIMAL not percent — 22% APR is 0.22, NOT 22.
Minimum $#,##0.00 What your lender requires per month. The Snowball + Avalanche cascade uses this. Use the printed minimum, not what you usually pay.
Due Day 1-31 Drives the Late-Fee Alert KPIs ("debts at risk in the next 5 days"). Day-of-month only, not a full date.
Target Payoff mmm yyyy Your aspiration date. Compared to the Strategy Comparison projection. Optional — leave blank if you're not sure yet.
Promo Ends mmm yyyy If this is a 0% promo, when does it reset? Avalanche re-prioritizes when ≤6 mo away. Read the statement carefully — promos end on a specific day, not "after 12 months."
Post APR 0.2599 = 25.99% The rate the debt resets to after promo ends. Decimal format like the APR field. Usually buried in the fine print — search "go-to APR" or "deferred interest."
Notes Free text Anything you want surfaced when you scan the Debt List. Keep it tight — long notes hide the math.
Plan Settings (below the totals row)
Income (K40): Your gross monthly income, pre-tax. This is the denominator for Debt-to-Income (DTI) — surfaced on the top-bar KPI strip. CFPB norms: ≤28% housing-only, ≤36% total. Above 43% triggers most mortgage declines. The Essentials and Pro Dashboards swap the Health Score block for a DTI block when income is set.
Snowball vs. Avalanche — pick the one you'll finish
❄️ Snowball — pick this if…
…you've started and stopped a payoff plan at least twice before. Or your smallest debt is <$2,000 and you'd like to see it gone in <3 months.
Math: pays off the smallest balance first. Costs ~$200–$1,000 more in total interest than Avalanche on a typical mix — buys you the momentum to actually finish.
🌊 Avalanche — pick this if…
…your highest APR is >20% and your balances are roughly comparable. Or you've already finished a payoff plan and you know you'll grind through.
Math: kills the highest-APR debt first. Saves the most total interest. On the standard 5-debt $39,700 portfolio: ~$450 less interest than Snowball.

Three creditor scripts that actually work
01
"Can you waive this late fee?"
If your account is >2 years old and otherwise clean, ask. Success rate: ~60% on the first ask, 80% on the second. Tell them you're enrolled in autopay now. Get the rep's name. Note it in the Debt List.
02
"Can you lower my APR?"
Works best on cards you've held 12+ months. Mention a 0% promo offer you got in the mail. Many issuers will drop 2-5 percentage points to keep the balance. Document the new rate in the APR column.
03
"I want to settle this for less."
For charged-off / collection debts only. Open the AI Credit Coach (AI Edition) — prompt #6 generates the letter for you. Heads up: a 1099-C may follow ($600+ forgiven debt is taxable income).
When things go sideways
Missed a payment? Lost income? Don't hide from the spreadsheet — open it. The Late-Fee Alert + Inquiry & Marks tabs (AI Edition) help you track damage and recover. Goodwill letters work on first-late marks.
