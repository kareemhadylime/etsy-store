Three Things People Wish They'd Known on Day One
01
Don't watch the live price.
20-minute delayed feed refreshes every minute — endless temptation to react. The Dashboard recomputes when you change Asset Allocation targets or add positions. Update positions monthly, not daily. Markets move 1-3% in a day; that's noise.
02
Wash-sale rule is across ALL accounts.
Sell at a loss in taxable + your IRA bought the same security in last 30 days = IRS disallows the loss permanently. Includes spouse's accounts. The AI TLH Scout prompt walks every recent buy across every account before recommending the sell. Be paranoid here.
03
REITs belong in Roth IRA.
REIT dividends are non-qualified — taxed as ordinary income at your marginal rate (24-37% federal + state). Same REIT in Roth IRA = tax-free forever. The Dividend Optimizer prompt (AI Edition page 8) flags this misplacement specifically — usually saves $40-200/year per misplaced position.
