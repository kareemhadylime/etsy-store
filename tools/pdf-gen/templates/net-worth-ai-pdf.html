How to use these prompts

Seven decisions every net-worth tracker hits across a year. Seven prompts you can copy, paste, and adapt — designed for the free tier of your favourite AI assistant.

Each prompt is built to pair with a specific tab in your Net Worth Tracker. You'll see the tab name on each page — paste, fill in placeholders, send.

How it works
  1. Open the prompt page you need (Monthly Narrative, FIRE Forecaster, etc.).
  2. Open the matching tab in your spreadsheet.
  3. Copy the prompt into your favourite AI assistant.
  4. Replace the [PLACEHOLDERS] with your data.
  5. Read the worked example on the same page to see what good output looks like.
What you'll need
  • An account with any AI assistant (the free tier works)
  • Your Net Worth Tracker open in another window
  • 10 minutes the first time; ~3 minutes once familiar
What this PDF won't do

It won't move money. It won't sell your gold, rebalance your 401k, file your estate paperwork, or call your insurance broker. You paste, you read, you decide. The AI proposes the move; you execute it in your brokerage, bank, or attorney's office.

Your account numbers and SSN never enter any AI tool. Use the spreadsheet's labels ("Brokerage Roth IRA," "Investment property #2") instead of account identifiers. Everything happens in your own AI account.

Turn the page when you're ready.

1. Monthly NW Narrative
🏠Pairs with: Dashboard
The Prompt
You are writing the one-paragraph story of what happened to my net worth this month. Explain the change in plain English. Separate market movement from my actions. THIS MONTH'S DATA (paste from Dashboard + NW History tab): - Net worth start of month: $[amount] - Net worth end of month: $[amount] - Change: $[amount] ($/% MoM) - Year-to-date change: $[amount] ($/% YTD) MOVES THIS MONTH (paste from NW History driver-breakdown): - Savings contributions across accounts: $[amount] - Debt principal paid down: $[amount] - Market gain/loss on equities: $[amount] - Real estate value change (if revalued): $[amount] - Metals/crypto change: $[amount] - Large one-off events: [list] For this month, write: 1. ONE-PARAGRAPH NARRATIVE (60-90 words) — what happened in plain English. 2. SIGNAL VS. NOISE — which portion was YOUR action vs. MARKET MOVEMENT. Express as approximate %. 3. ONE THING TO REPEAT next month (the highest-impact action you took). 4. ONE THING TO WATCH NEXT MONTH (the variable most likely to move your number). Skip motivational closings. Skip "consult a financial advisor." This is a journal entry, not a sales pitch.
Worked Example
Example Input

Start $312,400 · End $326,180 · Change +$13,780 (+4.4%) MoM · YTD +$28,900 (+9.7%)

Savings $2,200 · Debt paydown $1,150 · Market gain $9,800 · RE $0 · Metals +$280 · Crypto +$350 · Signing bonus $2,000 → HYSA

Example Output
NARRATIVE:

Your net worth jumped $13,780 this month, and most of that — about 73% — came from the market doing the lifting. Broad equities rallied roughly 4%, adding $9,800 to your accounts without you touching anything. Your own moves contributed about $3,350: $2,200 in fresh savings (including the signing bonus) and $1,150 in debt principal. Metals and crypto added small gains. A good month, but you'd be wise to remember whose work showed up here.

SIGNAL VS. NOISE:

YOUR ACTIONS: ~24% ($3,350) — savings + debt paydown · MARKET: ~76% ($10,430) — equity rally + metals + crypto

THING TO REPEAT:

The signing-bonus → HYSA flow. Treating windfalls as savings deposits (not "found money") is the discipline that compounds. Do this for every bonus, refund, gift.

THING TO WATCH:

Equity prices. If the market gives back 4% next month, $9,800 disappears from this snapshot. Your savings + paydown rate is the only number you control — keep eyes there, not on the daily NW value.

2. FIRE Forecaster
🔥Pairs with: FIRE Calculator
The Prompt
You are running the math on when I can reach Financial Independence under three scenarios. Be specific about years + months + the assumption swings that change the answer. MY CURRENT POSITION (paste from FIRE Calculator + Dashboard): - Current investable net worth (excludes primary residence): $[amount] - Current annual spend (after tax): $[amount] - Current monthly savings rate: $[amount]/month - Age: [number] - Target FIRE multiple: [25x = 4% rule / 28x = more conservative / 33x = ultra] - Expected post-FIRE spend (if different): $[amount] ASSUMPTIONS: - Real return: [4% conservative / 6% current / 8% aggressive] - Inflation: 2.5% - Social Security: [include from 67+ / exclude] Run THREE scenarios: SCENARIO A — CONSERVATIVE: 4% real, flat savings. SCENARIO B — CURRENT TRAJECTORY: 6% real, flat savings. SCENARIO C — AGGRESSIVE: 8% real + savings climbs $200/mo every 2 years. For each: 1. FIRE number, 2. Years + months to reach, 3. Age at FIRE, 4. Annual NW change needed. Then a TWO-PARAGRAPH HONEST READ — which is realistic, what the A-B gap means, what variable moves the answer most. No advisor-disclaimer ending.
Worked Example
Example Input

NW $284K · spend $58K · savings $1,850/mo · age 37 · 25x · SS at 67+ included

Example Output

FIRE NUMBER: $58K × 25 = $1,450,000

Scenario A — Conservative (4%):

22 years 3 months → age 59. Annual NW change ~$52,800/yr.

Scenario B — Current (6%):

17 years 1 month → age 54. Annual NW change ~$68,200/yr avg.

Scenario C — Aggressive (8% + glide):

13 years 4 months → age 50. Annual NW change ~$87,300/yr avg.

HONEST READ:

Scenario B is your realistic anchor — 6% real is a defensible historical assumption. Plan against age 54. The 5-year gap between A and B is mostly RETURNS — not yours to control. The 4-year gap between B and C is mostly SAVINGS RATE — yours to control.

The variable that moves the answer most is your savings rate. Adding $500/mo shaves about 2 years off every scenario. Returns are unknowable; savings rate is decided every month at the brokerage transfer.

3. Asset Allocation Advisor
📈Pairs with: Asset Allocation
The Prompt
You are diagnosing whether my asset allocation has drifted from my target — and if so, what to rebalance and how. MY ACTUAL CURRENT ALLOCATION (paste from Asset Allocation tab): [Asset class | Current $ | Current % | Target %] - US Equities, International Equities, Bonds, Real Estate, Metals, Crypto, Cash, Other MY CONTEXT: - Age, Years to FIRE, Last rebalance date, Recent large drifters, Constraints (TLH ops, 401k rebalance lock, unrealized gains in taxable). For each asset class: 1. ABSOLUTE DRIFT (current % - target %), 2. STATUS (✅ ±2pp / 🟡 2-5pp / 🔴 >5pp), 3. CAUSE (price, depreciation, neglected contributions, life event). Then: 1. RANK drifts by significance. 2. RECOMMEND 1-3 rebalancing moves with $ amounts. 3. FLAG tax implications. 4. EXPLAIN trade-off if selling appreciated holdings. Don't recommend financial advisor services. Don't recommend leveraged ETFs. Don't "diversify into alts."
Worked Example
Example Input

Total $284K · US Eq $148K/52% target 45% · Intl $34K/12% target 15% · Bonds $14K/5% target 10% · RE $43K/15% target 15% · Metals 7%/5% · Crypto 3.5%/2% · Cash 4%/5% · Other 1.5%/3%

Age 37 · 17yr to FIRE · last rebalance 8mo ago · US Eq +18% YTD · $14K unrealized gains in taxable; prefer contribution-rebalance

Example Output
RANKED DRIFTS:

1. US Equities overweight +7pp (+$20K over target) · 2. Bonds underweight -5pp (-$14K) · 3. International -3pp (-$8.5K)

RECOMMENDED MOVES:

Move 1 (THIS MONTH): Redirect next 4 months of new contributions ($2,200 × 4 = $8,800) entirely to bonds via 401k + Roth IRA. Closes the gap to within 2pp without selling. No tax hit.

Move 2 (NEXT MONTH): Redirect 2 months of contributions ($4,400) to international equities.

Move 3 (DEFER): US Eq overweight stays. Don't sell appreciated taxable shares — you'd realize $14K LTCG = ~$2,100 in federal tax.

ONE THING NOT TO DO:

Don't rebalance via leveraged inverse-equity ETFs to "hedge." They're not designed for multi-month holding; you'd compound your way to losses.

The Prompt
You are building me a realistic passive-income profile from my current portfolio and projecting monthly income today + at FIRE. Current gross monthly passive income per source. 2. Total after taxes (22% federal default). 3. % of monthly spend covered. For PROJECTED FIRE-YEAR: 1. Expected monthly under 3 SWR scenarios. 2. Shortfall or surplus vs target. 3. The ONE income lever with most upside. End with TWO PRACTICAL MOVES this year. Specific to actual holdings.
Worked Example
Example Input

Div ETFs $42K @ 3.4% · BND $14.2K @ 4.1% · VNQ $8K @ 3.8% · Rental net $1,170/mo · HYSA $11.4K @ 4.5% · Target $4,800/mo · FIRE 2043 · SS included

Example Output
TODAY'S PROFILE:

Gross monthly $1,405 (Div $119 + Bonds $48 + REIT $25 + Rental $1,170 + HYSA $43). After tax ~$1,135/mo. Covers 24% of $4,800 spend.

FIRE-YEAR PROFILE (at $1.45M portfolio):

4% SWR: $4,833/mo gross → ~$3,770 net · 4.5%: ~$4,241 net · 5%: ~$4,712 net

+ Rental $1,200/mo (2043) + SS $1,800/mo from age 67. By age 54: $5,441/mo net (surplus $641 — thin). By age 67: ~$7,200/mo.

ONE LEVER WITH MOST UPSIDE:

Your rental property. Already 84% of gross passive today. Doubling down (acquire rental #2 via cash-out refi in 5-8 years) shifts FIRE math more than any other single move.

TWO MOVES THIS YEAR:

1. Move BND from taxable → Roth IRA when room allows. Saves ~$130/yr in taxes; grows.

2. Open a "Rental Property #2 Fund" sinking fund. Target $40K down in 6 years at $550/mo.

5. Wealth Growth Coach
🤖Pairs with: AI Hub
The Prompt
You are looking at my full net-worth picture and naming the ONE move that has the biggest impact over the next 12 months. Not five moves. One. MY FULL PICTURE (paste from Dashboard + Annual Summary): - Current NW · Annual income · Annual savings rate · Debt totals · Largest asset class · Largest gap · Tax-advantaged room not used · Major life events expected next 12 mo MY GOAL FOR NEXT 12 MO: [grow NW $X / reach FIRE % / pay off debt / build EF / increase passive income / other] Pick the ONE MOVE with most NW impact. Then: 1. NAME THE MOVE — specific, executable in one sentence. 2. ESTIMATED 12-MO NW IMPACT in dollars. 3. WHY THIS ONE — what makes it higher-leverage. 4. WHAT IT TRADES OFF — what you're choosing not to do. 5. THE FIRST STEP this week. 6. THE 90-DAY CHECKPOINT — how I know it's working. Don't recommend 5 things. Don't say "all of these matter." Pick ONE. Be confident.
Worked Example
Example Input

NW $326K · Income $108K · Savings $22,200/yr · Mortgage $268K @ 5.5% · Student $14,200 @ 4.8% · US Eq 52% (gap +7pp) · 401k $8K under max · Roth $1,500 under · HSA $1,200 under · 2nd kid in 18mo

Goal: Grow NW $40K in 12mo.

Example Output
THE ONE MOVE:

Max your 401k for the year — add $8,000 of contribution by year-end.

12-MO NW IMPACT:

$8,000 fresh contribution + $1,760 tax savings (22% bracket) + $400-800 growth = ~$10,000-10,500 NW impact for $8,000 spent.

WHY THIS ONE:

(1) Tax leverage — pretax dollars = $10,256 of "real" wealth. (2) Time-of-life window — 2nd kid in 18mo means tighter cash flow. Max now while income is high. (3) Loss-of-room is permanent.

FIRST STEP THIS WEEK:

Log into 401k portal. Calculate % bump needed for remaining paychecks to hit $23,000. Submit change today.

90-DAY CHECKPOINT:

Day 90 YTD contribution should be ~$5,800 more. If behind pace, year-end lump-sum from HYSA.

6. Annual Wealth Review
📊Pairs with: Annual Summary
The Prompt
You are writing my year-end wealth review. Look at the full 12 months and tell me what worked, what didn't, and what to change next year. ANNUAL DATA (paste from Annual Summary tab): - NW start/end of year, change $/%, best/worst month, savings, debt paydown, market gain, real estate, metals/crypto, big one-offs. PROGRESS TOWARD FIRE: % funded start/end, pp change. PER-CLASS PERFORMANCE: Best, worst, largest rebalance moves. YoY: NW change, savings rate change. Give me: 1. THREE WINS — actions or outcomes that went better than planned. 2. THREE MISSES — outcomes that fell short. Honest diagnosis, not pep talk. 3. THREE LESSONS — structural patterns I now see. 4. THREE PRIORITIES NEXT YEAR — specific actions with $ targets. 5. ONE STRUCTURAL CHANGE. Each section under 80 words. No motivational ending.
Worked Example
Example Input

NW $284K → $342K (+$58.5K / +20.6%). Best March +$8.4K · Worst Oct -$3.2K · Savings $22.2K · Debt $14K · Market gain $26.8K · Bonus $2K + tax refund $1.4K + roof -$4.2K · FIRE 19.6% → 23.6% · Best US Eq +21% · Worst Bonds +0.4% · Rebalances: Mar-Jun bonds via contributions

Example Output
THREE WINS:

1. Mortgage principal paydown crossed $10K (11mo ahead of original amortization). 2. Contribution-rebalancing into bonds worked — closed gap without tax hit. 3. Signing bonus + refund went to investments instead of lifestyle creep.

THREE MISSES:

1. Oct roof killed $4.2K of NW; sinking fund underfunded ($800). 2. Savings rate flat — raise absorbed by lifestyle creep. 3. International equity drifted further (12% → 11%) — never directed new money there.

THREE LESSONS:

1. 2x YoY came mostly from the market, not you. Strip $26.8K and growth = $31.7K (~last year). 2. Sinking funds are NW protection. 3. Raises are NW opportunities — yours wasn't.

THREE PRIORITIES NEXT YEAR:

1. Savings rate $1,850 → $2,250/mo (raise → savings). 2. Top up home maintenance fund to $5K by June. 3. Max 401k ($8K under, ~$10K NW impact via tax leverage).

ONE STRUCTURAL CHANGE:

Move BND $14.2K from taxable → Roth IRA over 2 tax years. Saves ~$130/yr tax drag. Small, permanent fix.

7. Estate Planning Advisor
🤝Pairs with: Estate Access
The Prompt
You are auditing my estate-paperwork readiness. Not preparing documents — identifying which paperwork I have, which I'm missing, what's at risk. MY DOCUMENTATION STATUS (paste from Estate Access tab): - Will, Trust, Healthcare directive, POA, Beneficiary designations (401k/IRA/Roth/HSA/Brokerage TOD/Life ins/Bank POD), Asset-location reference, Trusted-angel contact, LTC insurance. MY HOUSEHOLD CONTEXT: - Age, marital status, dependents (ages), state, estate value, out-of-state real estate, special circumstances (special-needs dependent, blended family, non-citizen spouse, business equity, digital assets). For my situation: 1. PAPERWORK PRIORITY MATRIX — ✓ have / ✗ missing / ⚠️ stale (>3yr). 2. THE THREE HIGHEST-RISK GAPS — most likely to cause survivor problems. 3. EACH GAP'S CONSEQUENCE — what specifically goes wrong. 4. THREE THINGS IN NEXT 90 DAYS — ordered by urgency. 5. WHEN TO HIRE A LAWYER vs. DIY (specific to my complexity). Don't recommend specific attorneys or insurance products. Don't push financial-advisor estate bundles. Be honest about DIY-vs-pro thresholds.
Worked Example
Example Input

Will: draft 2yr (unexecuted) · No trust · No healthcare directive · No POA · 401k beneficiary 4yr old (pre-2nd-kid) · No HSA beneficiary · No brokerage TOD · No bank POD · No asset-location ref · Partial digital legacy (spouse has PW manager only)

Age 37 · Married · Kids 4 + 2 · California · Estate $342K · Texas rental · Business equity $28K

Example Output
THE THREE HIGHEST-RISK GAPS:

1. Unexecuted will + missing guardianship. Two minor kids, no signed guardianship. If both parents die intestate, CA probate court picks the guardian — not you.

2. Out-of-state rental + no trust. Texas rental goes through TX probate, separately from CA. Two probate processes, 4-8% asset value, 6-18 months each.

3. Stale 401k beneficiary. Beneficiary designations override your will. If 401k still names "spouse + child" (singular), your 2nd child may inherit nothing from this account.

THREE THINGS IN NEXT 90 DAYS:

Day 1-7: Update 401k + HSA beneficiaries. 20 minutes. Free.

Day 30-60: Execute the draft will OR redo via DIY (Trust & Will / LegalZoom $159-399). Include executor, guardianship, beneficiary clauses. Notarize + witness.

Day 60-90: Hire estate attorney for revocable trust + Texas rental retitling. Out-of-state property + business equity pushes you off DIY. $1,500-3,000 saves survivors $15K+ in probate.

DIY VS. ATTORNEY:

DIY: will, healthcare directive, POA, beneficiary updates. Attorney: revocable trust + out-of-state property retitling. DON'T buy LTC insurance at 37.

Which AI should I use?

All seven prompts work in free tiers. Differences come down to output style + how each handles long, structured wealth data.

Conversational, follow-up editing

Best for: Monthly NW Narrative, Wealth Growth Coach, Annual Wealth Review, Estate Planning Advisor. Most AI assistants have a conversational tone and are great at one-paragraph narratives + "pick ONE thing" judgement calls.

Limit: Free tiers cap how much you can paste per message (~3,000-4,000 words). If you paste 15+ holdings, do it in batches.

Longer structured input

Best for: FIRE Forecaster, Asset Allocation Advisor, Passive Income Blueprint. Some assistants are better at multi-scenario math, structured tables, and per-class drift analysis, and handle longer position lists in one paste.

Limit: Free tiers often cap conversations per day. If you hit it, switch to another AI assistant.

Paid tiers

Worth it if you're running multi-scenario FIRE math monthly OR managing 7+ accounts with quarterly rebalances. Skip if checking in twice a year.

Universal tips
  1. Paste as text, not screenshots. Free-tier AI doesn't read spreadsheet screenshots well.
  2. Never paste account numbers, SSN, brokerage logins, or attorney names. Use labels ("Brokerage Roth IRA," "Texas Rental").
  3. FIRE Forecaster works best with conservative assumptions. If the AI quotes 8% real returns confidently, downshift to 6% and re-ask.
  4. Estate prompt is diagnosis, not advice. The AI surfaces gaps. Filling them is your attorney's job.
  5. Save good output. Paste sharp diagnoses into the matching tab's Notes column. Build your own playbook over the year.

Your wealth data never leaves your AI conversation. The AI never sees your spreadsheet — only what you paste, only during that chat.
