Three Things People Wish They\'d Known Before Their First Zakat
01
Pick one Nisab method, stick with it.
Don\'t toggle Gold/Silver year-to-year based on which produces a lower obligation — that\'s optimizer-thinking, not Zakat practice. NZF UK + AAOIFI: consistent method over years is the recommendation across all 4 Madhhabs.
02
Pay before Eid Salah for Fitr.
Zakat al-Fitr must reach recipients BEFORE the Eid prayer to fulfill the obligation in its proper time. NZF UK §8.4: pay 1-3 days before Eid for safety. After Eid Salah, it becomes Sadaqah (voluntary), not Zakat al-Fitr.
03
Don\'t skip Gharimin (debtors).
Most retail Zakat-payers go to global orgs and skip Gharimin entirely. But this is where direct, person-to-person Zakat is most legitimate per Quranic basis (At-Tawbah 9:60). Use mosque committee to identify a recipient anonymously.
