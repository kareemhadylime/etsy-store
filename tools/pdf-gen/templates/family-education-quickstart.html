Three Things Parents Wish They'd Known on Day One
01
529 wins for ~85% of families.
Coverdell phases out at moderate income. UTMA loses parental control at 18-21 + counts heavier on FAFSA. ABLE is for special-needs only. Default is 529; the Account Type Comparison tab tells you when the default doesn't apply (Noah's case — autism → ABLE).
02
In-state usually beats out-of-state.
NY's $342/yr deduction compounds to $4,500+ over 18 years. Most state plans win for residents. Only go out-of-state (Utah my529) if your home plan has >0.5% expense ratios AND no deduction. State 529 Tax Benefits tab does the math for all 50 states + DC.
03
Term life beats whole life 15× cheaper.
$1.25M of 25-yr term at age 36 ≈ $60/mo. Same coverage in whole life ≈ $1,000/mo. The agent-pitched "whole life for college" loses to 529 + term on the after-tax math. The ONE legit whole-life scenario is special-needs-trust funding. AI Life Insurance Advisor (PDF page 5) explains why.
