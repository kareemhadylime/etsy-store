Three Things People Wish They'd Known on Day One
01
Vehicle matters more than discipline.
A 4-year Down Payment fund in HYSA earns 4.5%. The same fund in a 60/40 ETF/bond mix earns 6–8% expected. Over $25K × 4 years that's $2,000–3,000. Pick the right vehicle FIRST; auto-contribute SECOND.
02
Auto-contribute or the fund decays.
Manual top-ups fail beyond 3 months. Every "I'll add to it next month" is a fund you'll forget by month 6. Set autopay from checking → fund account on payday. Neglected Fund Detector (AI) catches the ones you missed.
03
Glide volatile vehicles 24 months out.
Stock-funded Down Payment with 12 months to closing is one bad quarter from missing the date. The Wealth Glide Path tab tells you exactly when to shift volatile → safe. You stay in control of trades; we flag the deadlines.

Page 2 · Setup Walkthrough

Filling the Fund Manager, field by field.

The single tab that drives every other number in the workbook. Get this right and the rest is automatic.
Time
~3 min
The 10 fields on each fund row
FieldFormatWhy it mattersWatch out for
Fund Name Free text Identifies the fund across every tab. Use the name your future self will recognize ("Maui 2027" not "Travel"). Avoid account numbers — use the goal, not the bank.
Category Dropdown (17) Drives Tax Efficiency placement (Medical → HSA, Education → 529) + 17-category match. Pick from the list — Custom is fine, but loses smart tax placement.
Vehicle Dropdown (5) Cash / CD / ETF / Metal / Stock. Drives the 4-vehicle Dashboard donut + per-vehicle tracker tabs. Match horizon to vehicle: under 1yr → Cash; 5+yr → ETF/Stock.
Target $#,##0 Where you want this fund to land. Drives % funded + Priority Matrix scoring. Be specific — "Maui trip $3,000" beats "Travel some amount."
Saved $#,##0 Your current balance for this fund. Updates as you log contributions. Keep this fresh — stale balances = wrong status pills.
Target Date mmm d, yyyy When you need the money. Drives months-remaining + Status pill calc. Leave blank for "forever" funds (Emergency Fund) — Status shows "—".
Monthly $#,##0 What you contribute per month. Status pill compares this to required-to-hit-target. Use actual auto-deposit amount, not aspirational.
Growth % 0.045 = 4.5% Expected annual return. Decimal format. HYSA 0.045 · CD 0.048 · ETF 0.065 · Metals 0. DECIMAL not percent — 4.5% APY is 0.045, NOT 4.5.
Status Auto-calculated 🟢 Funded / 🟢 On Track / 🟡 At Risk / 🔴 Behind. Pulled from monthly contribution vs. required. Don't edit — formula recomputes when you change Target/Saved/Monthly/Date.
Notes Free text Anything you want surfaced when you scan the Fund Manager. Keep it tight — long notes hide the math.
Setup Section (rows 6–7, above the fund table)
Three one-time choices: Currency (USD default · drives $ formatting across all tabs) · Household mode (Solo / Shared · toggles Pro features) · Metals Live (ON / OFF · enables GOOGLEFINANCE spot-price lookups in Sheets only · Excel users keep OFF and enter spot prices manually monthly).
Pick a vehicle — match horizon to volatility
💵 Cash / HYSA — pick this if…
…the fund is under 12 months from target. Emergency Fund · Christmas · Vacation · Wedding Gift · Car Repair (annual). 4–5% APY, liquid, zero risk.
Math: a HYSA at 4.5% on $9,000 = $405/yr. Same money in checking earns ~$5. The Vehicle Advisor AI prompt names the wrong-placement funds in your sheet.
📈 ETF / Stock — pick this if…
…the fund is 3+ years out and you can stomach 20–30% short-term drawdowns. Down Payment · Retirement supplement · long-term college.
Math: 60/40 ETF/bond on $25K over 4 years vs HYSA = ~$2,000–3,000 difference. But: glide to cash starting T-24 months per the Wealth Glide Path tab. CDs fill the 1–3yr middle.

Three vehicle moves that actually pay off
01
Emergency Fund: checking → HYSA.
Move it this week. A $9,000 EF sitting in checking earns ~$5/year. The same money in a 4.5% HYSA earns $405/year — for the same liquidity. Ally / Marcus / Discover all offer no-minimum HYSAs. 10-minute setup.
02
Multi-year funds: HYSA → CD Ladder.
Got a Down Payment with 3-year horizon? Stagger 4 CDs (12 / 18 / 24 / 36 mo) so one matures each year. CD APYs run 0.3–0.5 ppt above HYSA. Use the CD Ladder tab — FV @ maturity formula is auto-calculated.
03
Long-horizon: ETF in taxable, not Roth.
5+ year fund in 60/40 ETF/bond mix outperforms HYSA by ~$2K–3K over 5 years on a $25K base. Use taxable brokerage — don't burn Roth IRA room on a Down Payment when retirement needs that space.
When you withdraw
Use the Spending Tracker (Pro+) to log withdrawals. Rebuild Timeline column tells you exactly how many months at current contribution rate to replace what you pulled. Don't beat yourself up — funds exist to be spent. Just log the spend.
What we are NOT
This isn't investment advice or tax advice. The Vehicle Advisor + Tax Efficiency tabs give educated defaults; your situation may differ. For complex tax moves (529 / HSA optimization beyond contribution limits), consult a CPA.
