Set them up in this order.
Why the order matters: data flows downstream. Set them up out of order and you'll fix the same fields twice.
Time
~45 min
The principle. Budget Tracker is the foundation — it defines your income, fixed expenses, and saving capacity. Sinking Funds and Debt Payoff borrow Budget's categories. Net Worth pulls balances from Debt Payoff and from your bank/account list. Small Business is independent but its net profit feeds back into Budget. Set up in the wrong order and you'll re-enter categories, re-classify expenses, and rebuild the same plan twice.
1
📊 Budget Tracker
Foundation. Defines income, fixed expenses, category structure, savings capacity. Every other product borrows from here.
2
🪣 Sinking Funds
Borrows monthly available-to-sink from Budget. Set up after Budget so you know the real capacity, not aspirational.
3
💰 Net Worth Tracker
Pulls liability balances from Debt Payoff and asset balances from your account list. Needs all account names in place.

Mostly standalone. Its monthly net profit feeds back into Budget as a separate "Side Business" income line. Set up last; revisit Budget after first month to wire profit back.
Each product has an Input tab and an Output Dashboard. Input tabs are where you enter data (your numbers, your accounts, your debts). Output Dashboards visualize what you entered. You only ever edit the Input tab. The Dashboard updates automatically.

Page 9 · Troubleshooting + Support
When something doesn't add up.
Five issues we see most often, with the one-line fix for each. If you don't see your problem here, email us.
Need help
24-hr reply
My Net Worth and Debt Payoff debt totals don't match.
You probably updated one but not the other after this month's payment. Set the first Saturday of every month as your "sync day" — open both, copy the current balance from Debt Payoff into Net Worth Liabilities. 30 seconds, done.
My Budget says I have $500/mo to save but Sinking Funds shows the funds are behind.
Sinking Funds calculates required-monthly from target dates. If you allocated $500 across 6 funds but the math says you need $720 to hit all targets, that's a real shortfall. Use the Priority Matrix to decide which fund slips its target date.
Small Business profit is showing up but Budget income doesn't reflect it.
Small Business doesn't auto-push to Budget. End of each month, open the Small Business P&L, note net profit, then open Budget Income tab and add a "Side Business" line for that amount. Manual by design — gives you control over what counts as income.
I changed a category name in Budget — now Sinking Funds shows the old name.
Sinking Funds borrowed Budget's category list at setup. They don't auto-sync. If you rename "Pets" to "Childcare" in Budget, also rename it in Sinking Funds Fund Manager. Reverse is true too — rename in one place, then mirror to the other.
Excel formulas show #VALUE! or #REF! errors when I open in Sheets.
Some Excel functions don't translate (mainly array formulas). Use File → Import → Replace spreadsheet instead of opening the .xlsx directly. Google's importer handles the formula translation. If a specific cell still errors, email us with the cell address — we ship a patch.
My Dashboard KPIs show $0 even though I entered data.
Dashboard KPIs read named cells, not column references. If you inserted a row at the top of an Input tab, you shifted the named cells. Undo with Ctrl+Z, then add rows below the data block, not above. (Both Excel and Sheets respect named ranges automatically if you add rows in the middle.)
