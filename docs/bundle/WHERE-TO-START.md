# Where to Start — Bundle Decision Tree

You bought 6 spreadsheets. Don't open all of them. Pick ONE based on your current situation.

## The 1-question filter

**What's keeping you up at night about money right now?**

| If you said... | Open this first | Skip until later |
|---|---|---|
| "I owe too much" | Debt Payoff Planner | All others — debt is the only number that matters when minimum payments exceed your savings rate |
| "I have no idea where my money goes" | Budget Tracker | All others — you can't optimize what you can't measure |
| "I have specific goals (vacation, house, car, wedding) and need to know how much to save monthly" | Sinking Funds Planner | All others — these are bounded problems with deadlines |
| "I'm doing fine month-to-month but want to know if I'm building wealth" | Net Worth Tracker | All others — start with a balance sheet, not a flow |
| "I have a brokerage account / 401k and want to track performance, allocation, risk" | Investment Portfolio Tracker | Net Worth Tracker can wait until later |
| "I have kids and college is approaching" | Family & Education Planner | Other SKUs are personal; this one is for them |

## After the first 30 days

Once your first SKU is set up and used for a month, add the next one based on your trajectory.

### Trajectory A: Climbing out of debt
- Started with: Debt Payoff Planner
- Add next: Budget Tracker (to find the money for accelerated payoff)
- Then: Sinking Funds Planner (small emergency fund while paying down)
- Skip for now: NWT, IPT, F&E

### Trajectory B: Building savings habits
- Started with: Budget Tracker
- Add next: Sinking Funds Planner (turn savings into buckets with names)
- Then: Net Worth Tracker (start tracking momentum)
- Then: Investment Portfolio Tracker (when you have $5K+ to invest)

### Trajectory C: Wealth accumulation
- Started with: Net Worth Tracker
- Add next: Investment Portfolio Tracker (drill down on assets)
- Then: Sinking Funds Planner (for windfall management)
- Later: F&E Planner (if children in picture)

### Trajectory D: Family planning
- Started with: Family & Education Planner
- Add next: Net Worth Tracker (parents' balance sheet)
- Then: Sinking Funds Planner (for non-education goals)
- Then: Budget Tracker (to find the monthly contribution)

## Common starting mistakes

- **Opening all 6 the first day.** You'll abandon the bundle within a week. One SKU for 30 days; then add the next.
- **Picking the "fanciest" SKU first.** Investment Portfolio Tracker is the most feature-rich SKU but useless until you actually have investments. Don't start there for ego.
- **Trying to make the numbers match between SKUs before you've finished filling either.** Each SKU works standalone. Cross-reconciliation is a month-end discipline once you've used both for a month each.
- **Modifying formulas before reading the AI advisor PDF.** The PDF explains *why* the numbers are computed the way they are. If you change a formula without reading the advisor, you may break the methodology.

## Re-read this in 90 days

After 90 days of use, re-read this guide. Your situation will have changed. Maybe you started with Debt Payoff and you're now debt-free — that's the trigger to switch to Net Worth Tracker as your primary.

The 6-SKU bundle is designed for a 12-month progression. You don't need all 6 active at once — most customers will have 2-3 active SKUs at any given time.
