----------------------------------------------------------------
PROJECT GOAL
----------------------------------------------------------------
Sell AI-enhanced finance & life-organisation spreadsheet/template
products on Etsy, with a privately-hosted backend (Next.js +
Supabase + Vercel) that handles:
- Public storefront (links out to Etsy listings)
- Admin product catalogue + file delivery (signed URLs from Storage)
- Etsy receipt webhooks → customer/order records → email + files
- Server-side conversion tracking (Meta CAPI, GA4 MP, TikTok Events)
- Cross-platform analytics + ad metrics ingestion
- Content publishing engine (multi-platform)
- AI content generator (Claude for text, Nano Banana for images)

Every product follows a 3-tier model: Essentials / Pro / AI Edition.
