## Patterns

### Pricing power matters more than volume

Every item on this list breaks $500/wk by combining mid-to-high price ($10.71–$75) with 14+ weekly sales. **The $1 race-to-bottom budget trackers do not appear on this list** despite doing 91–411 sales/wk, because $0.99 × 91 = $90/wk.

### Three winning patterns

1. **Bundles** (5 of 10): "Bundle", "BIG Bundle", "Reading Journal Bundle", "Wildflower Wedding Bundle", "Wedding Bundle". Bundling → premium price → unlocks $500+/wk.
2. **Wedding** (4 of 10): Wedding planners and invitations are unusually price-tolerant ($16.60–$75).
3. **Notion / Premium Spreadsheet** (2 of 10): Premium tier finance/Notion templates at $11–$36 hitting $500–$1,700/wk.

### Volume distribution
- Top 1: $1,692/wk (one outlier)
- Tier 2 ($1,000–$1,600): 4 products (positions 2–5)
- Tier 3 ($500–$799): 5 products (positions 6–10)
- **Median weekly revenue at the top:** ~$868/wk
