## Key Additions

### ⏳ Receivables Aging (Customer Payments)
- Groups all outstanding invoices into buckets: Current / 1–30 days / 31–60 days / 61–90 days / 90+ days
- Total at risk per bucket
- Oldest unpaid invoice flagged
- Collection priority list auto-generated
- Cash impact: if 90+ bucket collected, what does cash position look like?

### ⏳ Payables Aging (Supplier Payments)
- Same aging structure for what YOU owe suppliers
- Due date calendar view
- Early payment discount tracker (some suppliers offer 2/10 net 30)
- Cash flow impact: total payables due in next 30/60/90 days

### 👥 HR Employee Records
- Employee profile: name, role, department, start date, contract type (full-time/part-time/contractor)
- Bank details, tax code/W4 filing status, emergency contact
- Holiday/leave entitlement and days taken
- Performance review dates

### 💰 Payroll & Payslips
- **Hourly mode**: hours worked × hourly rate + overtime (1.5×) = gross pay
- **Monthly mode**: annual salary ÷ 12 = gross pay (with proration for partial months)
- Deductions: FICA (6.2% SS + 1.45% Medicare), federal withholding, state/local tax, pension/401k contribution
- Net pay auto-calculated
- Auto-generates formatted payslip per employee per period (weekly or monthly)
- Up to 10 employees/contractors

### 🌐 Social Security Tracker
- Employer SS contribution (6.2% up to wage base) per employee per period
- Employee SS contribution matched
- Running annual SS total per employee
- Wage base threshold alert (2026: $176,100) — when employee hits cap, SS stops
- Medicare surcharge flag (0.9% additional above $200k)
- Quarterly 941 filing summary (ready to hand to accountant)
