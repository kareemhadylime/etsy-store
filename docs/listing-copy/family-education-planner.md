## 7. FAQs (10)

> **Q: How many children can I track?**
> A: Essentials supports up to 2 children. Pro and AI Edition support up to 4 children. Each child gets their own column across all relevant tabs (College Savings Planner, Account Type Comparison, K-12 Cost Map, Childcare Cost Planner, Scholarship Tracker, Financial Literacy Milestones). If you have more than 4 children, contact us — we'll send a custom version.

> **Q: 529 vs. UTMA vs. Coverdell vs. ABLE — which should I pick?**
> A: It depends per child. The Account Type Comparison tab (Essentials+) shows side-by-side contribution limits, FAFSA impact, spending flexibility, and investment control. AI Edition's Account Type Picker prompt recommends the right vehicle per child based on age, target school tier, your tax bracket, and special-needs considerations. Worked example in the AI PDF: 529 for one child + UTMA for another isn't unusual.

> **Q: What's the EFC / SAI Calculator actually doing?**
> A: It replicates the FAFSA formula (Pro+). Input your household income, assets, family size, and number of children in college — output is your likely Expected Family Contribution (or SAI under the new FAFSA system). The College Affordability Coach prompt (AI Edition) interprets the number against the colleges your child is considering and shows the actual cost gap to plan for. Verify your final EFC against the official FAFSA submission.

> **Q: Does the Scholarship Tracker actually find scholarships?**
> A: It tracks them — applied / won / pending / declined per child with deadline countdowns (Pro+). The AI Scholarship Matching Engine (AI Edition) gives you a student profile → 5–7 most-likely scholarships to research + positioning tips. We don't auto-apply or submit; you do the research and the applications. Free auto-apply services like ScholarshipOwl sell your data; we don't.

> **Q: 529 vs. Whole Life Insurance — why is this comparison even in the spreadsheet?**
> A: Because life-insurance salespeople routinely pitch whole-life policies as "college funding" to new parents — and the after-tax math almost always favors a 529. The 529 vs. Whole Life tab (Essentials+) shows after-tax cost, flexibility, break-even year, and tax benefit by bracket so you can have an informed conversation with whoever is pitching you. Honest math protects buyers.

> **Q: State 529 Tax Benefits — how does that work?**
> A: All 50 states are in a lookup table (Pro+). Select your residency state, and the tab shows your state's 529 deduction ($235–$540 annually for most states) and projects the compound benefit over 18 years. The State 529 Optimizer prompt (AI Edition) recommends in-state vs. out-of-state 529 plans — sometimes another state's plan has better funds even after losing your home-state deduction.

> **Q: Do I need a paid AI plan for AI features?**
> A: No. The 8 AI advisor prompts (AI Edition only) work on the free tier of your AI assistant. You paste the prompt + your data into your own AI tool. Nothing in the spreadsheet sends data to any AI — the AI part happens in your own browser, in your own account. Your kids' information stays on your device.

> **Q: Will it work if I'm planning for a second child?**
> A: Yes. The Family Goals Conflict Resolver prompt (AI Edition) is specifically designed for this scenario — it flags when planned timelines collide (e.g., new baby cost + first kid's college start + your retirement contributions). The Income Transition Buffer pattern is documented in the AI PDF worked examples for households planning a partner switching to part-time after a second child.

> **Q: What about blended families / special-needs / out-of-state college complexity?**
> A: All supported (Pro+). Per-child fields include school-type and target tier independently — so one child can be on a private K-12 + in-state public college track while another is public K-12 + out-of-state. ABLE accounts (in the Account Type Comparison tab) are specifically for special-needs dependents. The AI Account Type Picker handles these mixed scenarios in its per-child recommendation.

> **Q: What if I find a bug or want a feature?**
> A: Reply to your order email — 24-hour response. Bug fixes free forever. AI Edition buyers get 12 months of free template refreshes. Essentials and Pro are one-time purchases with free bug fixes only.
