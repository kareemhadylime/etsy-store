## 7. FAQs (10)

> **Q: Does this work in Excel?**
> A: No. This is Google Sheets only. The live-price feature uses GOOGLEFINANCE, which is a Sheets-exclusive function. Excel cannot pull live equity prices, FX rates, or metal spot prices without a paid third-party add-in. If you need Excel, this isn't the right product — try a manually-updated Excel portfolio tracker instead. We're transparent about this; some sellers ship "Excel + Sheets" versions where the Excel version is broken.

> **Q: How is "live" pricing actually live?**
> A: GOOGLEFINANCE pulls prices with a 20-minute delay during market hours and updates approximately every minute when the sheet is open. This is the same delayed feed used by most retail tools (Yahoo Finance, Bloomberg's retail tier). True real-time pricing requires a paid broker terminal or institutional data feed. We're honest about the delay; "real-time" apps also have it but don't disclose.

> **Q: Does it connect to my brokerage?**
> A: No. That's the privacy gate. You enter positions manually — ticker + shares + cost basis + account location. GOOGLEFINANCE handles the price column automatically. Most buyers spend 15-20 minutes per month updating positions vs. handing brokerage credentials to Plaid + giving Sharesight permanent read-only access to all accounts forever.

> **Q: How is this different from Sharesight, Stock Rover, Kubera?**
> A: Four things. (1) Price — $17-$34 once vs $96-$300/year forever. Sharesight + Stock Rover + Kubera combined = $596/year. Over 5 years that's $2,980 vs $34. (2) Privacy — no broker linking, no Plaid, no aggregator-server holding your full portfolio. (3) Depth — 10 asset classes including options/RSUs vesting (which most apps charge premium tiers for). (4) Custom — full spreadsheet control. Modify any formula, add tabs, change benchmark. Apps don't let you do this.

> **Q: How many positions can I track?**
> A: Essentials supports up to 30 positions. Pro and AI Edition support up to 60. If you have more than 60 positions, you're an unusually diversified retail investor — contact us, we'll send a custom version (free for AI Edition buyers).

> **Q: Risk metrics — Sharpe / Beta / Max Drawdown / Volatility — are these real or simplified?**
> A: Real (Pro+). Sharpe ratio uses your portfolio's returns vs risk-free rate (10-year Treasury) and standard deviation. Beta is calculated against your chosen benchmark (S&P 500 default; configurable to MSCI World, custom). Max drawdown uses your monthly NAV history. Volatility is annualized from monthly returns. The math is identical to what Sharesight and Stock Rover use — we just show it in a spreadsheet you can verify.

> **Q: Tax-Loss Harvesting — does it execute trades for me?**
> A: No (privacy + control gate). It surfaces opportunities. The Tax-Loss Harvesting tab (Pro+) tracks per-position cost basis vs current value, flags positions with unrealized losses, and shows the wash-sale window (30 days before/after a buy of the same or substantially identical security). The AI Edition Tax-Loss Harvesting Scout prompt suggests sell/buy pairs that avoid wash-sale violations. YOU execute the trades at your broker.

> **Q: Do I need ChatGPT Plus or Claude Pro for the AI features?**
> A: No. The 8 AI prompts (AI Edition only) work in ChatGPT free tier or Claude free tier. You paste the prompt + your data into your own AI tool. Nothing in the spreadsheet sends data to any AI — the AI part happens in your own browser, in your own account. The Quarterly Portfolio Review prompt + multi-position Look-Through Analyzer benefit from a paid tier's longer context window if you have 30+ positions, but free works for most users.

> **Q: Region tax toggle — what does that do?**
> A: AI Edition includes 5 regional tax-treatment modes: US (federal short-term/long-term + state add-ons) / UK (CGT + ISA wrappers) / EU (varies by member state, defaults to Germany rules) / AU (CGT + 12-month discount + super) / CA (50% inclusion rule + RRSP/TFSA wrappers). The Tax Lot Tracker and Tax-Loss Harvesting tabs adjust accounting rules based on your selection.

> **Q: What if I find a bug or want a feature?**
> A: Reply to your order email — 24-hour response. Bug fixes free forever. AI Edition buyers get 12 months of free template refreshes (tax law + IRS table changes auto-propagate). Essentials and Pro are one-time purchases with free bug fixes only.
