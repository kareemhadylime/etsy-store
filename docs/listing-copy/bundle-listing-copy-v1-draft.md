## Description

### Lead

Six personal-finance spreadsheets that work together. Not six apps. Not six subscriptions. Six Excel files (also Google-Sheets compatible) that you own forever — at the price of one app's monthly subscription.

### What you get

- **Budget Tracker** — Monthly income + expenses, 50/30/20 method, savings rate. Pro tier adds zero-based budgeting, sinking-fund integration. AI Edition adds an AI advisor that critiques your spending.
- **Debt Payoff Planner** — Snowball, Avalanche, and Custom payoff strategies side by side. Pro adds APR optimization. AI Edition simulates "what if I add $X/mo".
- **Sinking Funds Planner** — Up to 17 named savings buckets with target dates. Pro adds priority matrix. AI Edition adds goal-scoring + Wealth Glide Path.
- **Net Worth Tracker** — Assets - Liabilities, monthly history, debt-to-asset ratio. Pro adds FIRE calculator. AI Edition adds 10-currency FX, AI advisor.
- **Investment Portfolio Tracker** — Multi-currency holdings, allocation chart, dividend income calendar. Pro adds risk metrics. AI Edition adds scenario simulator (Recovery, FIRE Delay, Scenarios).
- **Family & Education Planner** — 529 planning, multi-child support, EFC/SAI calculator. Pro adds scholarship tracker. AI Edition adds K-12 cost mapping + state 529 benefits.

### Each SKU comes with

- An AI Advisor PDF (8-12 pages of methodology + recommendations specific to your situation)
- A Quickstart PDF (1 page to get up and running)
- 3 tier versions (Essentials / Pro / AI Edition — use whichever fits your needs)

### Bundle-only extras

- Bundle README + "Where to Start" decision tree (because 6 SKUs is overwhelming)
- 9-page Bundle Setup Wizard PDF
- 29-page AI Library PDF (reference for all 6 AI advisors)
- File manifest for download-integrity verification

### Why a bundle, not separate purchases?

The six SKUs share concepts: your "monthly savings" line is in your Budget Tracker, but the buckets that money flows into live in the Sinking Funds Planner. Your "investments" line in Net Worth Tracker is the total MV your Investment Portfolio Tracker computes. Buying them together saves [bundle-pricing-discount]% over buying separately and ensures every cross-reference works.

### What you DON'T need

- No subscription
- No credit-bureau handshake
- No bank-account linking
- No internet connection required (works fully offline in Excel)
- Your wealth data never leaves your device

### Compatibility

- Microsoft Excel 2016+ (Windows, Mac)
- Google Sheets (upload the .xlsx — see notes on GOOGLEFINANCE for live FX)
- LibreOffice Calc
- Apple Numbers (some advanced features limited)

### Recommended tier

If you're not sure, start with **AI Edition** — it has everything Pro has, plus an AI Advisor tab you can ignore until you're ready.
