## 7. FAQs (10)

> **Q: What is a sinking fund?**
> A: A pre-allocated savings bucket for a specific future expense. Christmas gifts in November. Car repair fund (because the AC will break). Wedding fund. Property tax. Instead of getting surprised in month 11, you save $X each month from month 1. This spreadsheet manages 17 categories of these by default; unlimited custom in Pro+.

> **Q: Is this a Google Sheet or an Excel file?**
> A: Google Sheets primary; Excel courtesy export included. Some Pro/AI features (live spot-price lookups for metals via GOOGLEFINANCE, multi-currency live FX) only work in Sheets. The core fund-tracking math works in both. If Excel is your primary tool, you'll need to manually update metals prices monthly.

> **Q: How is this different from Qapital, Monarch, or YNAB's goals?**
> A: Three things. (1) Privacy — your savings strategy lives in your Google Drive, not on a SaaS server. (2) Price — $9 once vs. Qapital $5/mo or Monarch $99/yr. Over 5 years, $271+ or $466+ saved. (3) Vehicle coverage — Qapital does round-ups in cash only. Monarch aggregates account balances but doesn't help you decide CD vs. ETF vs. metals. This spreadsheet handles all 4 vehicles + recommends placement (Tax Efficiency Analyzer in Pro).

> **Q: Can I really save in precious metals through this?**
> A: You can track your holdings in precious metals through this (gold/silver/platinum/palladium). The actual buying happens at your bullion dealer or brokerage. The spreadsheet tracks ounces × monthly spot price (live via GOOGLEFINANCE in Sheets), cost basis, storage location, and value evolution. If you don't hold metals, that tab stays empty — no penalty.

> **Q: I'm scared of CDs / stocks / ETFs. Do I need all 4 vehicles?**
> A: No. Use whichever ones fit your situation. Cash-only Sinking Funds buyers use 1 of the 4 trackers. Pro+ unlocks the other 3 if/when you grow into them. The Vehicle Advisor AI prompt (AI Edition) recommends the right vehicle per fund based on time horizon (under 12 months → cash/HYSA, 1–3 years → CD/short bond, 5+ years → ETF, etc.).

> **Q: What's the Neglected Fund Detector?**
> A: The AI Edition's secret weapon. The prompt scans your contribution history and flags goals you stopped contributing to without realizing — e.g., "You haven't added to the Wedding fund in 4 months; your target date is 8 months out; you're now $1,800 behind plan." Most people don't notice neglected goals until the deadline. This catches them at month 4, not month 12.

> **Q: Do I need a paid AI plan for the AI features?**
> A: No. The 7 AI advisor prompts (AI Edition only) work on the free tier of your AI assistant. You paste the prompt + your data into your own AI tool. Nothing in this spreadsheet sends data to any AI service — the AI part happens in your own browser, in your own account.

> **Q: Can I share with my partner / household?**
> A: Yes (Pro+). Household Mode supports shared funds, individual funds, and a settlement row. Both partners can edit via Google Sheets sharing — invite them with edit access. Essentials supports 1 user.

> **Q: Will this work for short-term goals (Christmas gifts) and long-term (down payment)?**
> A: Yes — that's the whole point. The Wealth Glide Path (Pro+) auto-recommends shifting from volatile (stocks) to safe (CDs/cash) as your target date approaches. So a Down Payment fund 5 years out might be 80% ETF; 18 months out, 50% bonds; 6 months out, 100% HYSA. You stay in control of trades; we just flag the rebalance moments.

> **Q: What if I find a bug or want a feature?**
> A: Reply to your order email — 24-hour response. Bug fixes free forever. AI Edition buyers get 12 months of free template refreshes; Essentials and Pro are one-time purchases with free bug fixes only.
