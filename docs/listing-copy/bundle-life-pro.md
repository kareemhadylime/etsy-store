## 7. FAQs (10)

> **Q: How is this different from the Premium Finance Bundle?**
> A: This Life Bundle = 6 spreadsheets including the Wedding Budget & Planner. The Finance Bundle = 5 spreadsheets, no Wedding ($79 Pro / $119 AI). If you have a wedding to plan (yours, your partner's, a kid's), this is the right bundle. If not, save $20 on the Finance Bundle.

> **Q: I'm not engaged yet — should I buy this or wait?**
> A: Buy now if you're saving for an engagement or wedding ahead — the Sinking Funds + Budget + Wedding combo is designed for the pre-engagement phase. The Wedding spreadsheet's Setup Wizard tab lets you stub a tentative date and adjust later when reality hits. Buying early means you start saving with structure, not after the venue deposit hits your card.

> **Q: We're already married — is this overkill on the wedding piece?**
> A: Honestly, maybe. If your wedding is behind you, the 5-product Finance Bundle ($97) covers everything except Wedding for $32 less. That said, the Wedding spreadsheet has an Annual Reflection tab and a Honeymoon Budget tab buyers do use post-wedding — and you might want it for a kid's or sibling's wedding eventually.

> **Q: What does "all-or-nothing refund" mean exactly?**
> A: If you ask for a refund, we revoke access to all 6 files. We can't refund 1/6 of the price and let you keep five — Etsy doesn't support that for digital products. If you're unsure, start with one or two standalone listings first to test fit.

> **Q: I already own the Wedding Budget & Planner. Can I get this bundle minus that?**
> A: Yes via coupon. Email us your Etsy order number for the Wedding purchase and we'll send a coupon code for the difference. Processed within 24 hours. Net: you don't pay twice.

> **Q: Setup wizard — what's in it?**
> A: 10 pages. Cover, setup-order rationale ("set them up in this order, here's why"), 1 page per spreadsheet with 3 first-actions, a cross-product reference diagram, and troubleshooting. The Wedding setup page has a dusty-rose header accent (the only visual difference from the Finance Bundle's wizard) — everything else is identical.

> **Q: Can I share the bundle with my partner?**
> A: Yes. Google Sheets has native sharing — invite your partner with edit access to any or all 6 sheets. Bundle purchase covers one Etsy buyer; sharing within your own household is normal.

> **Q: What about Investment Portfolio Tracker / Family & Education / Zakat Calculator?**
> A: Not included in this bundle. We considered a 9-SKU Mega Bundle but deferred to v2 — those three products serve smaller, distinct audiences (investors / parents-with-college-savings / Muslim buyers), so they're sold standalone.

> **Q: Excel or Google Sheets?**
> A: Google Sheets in v1. Excel exports ship as courtesy files inside the bundle download. Wedding's complex tabs (Seating Chart, Master Timeline) lose some Google Sheets-only functionality in Excel — check the individual Wedding listing's FAQ for current Excel-parity status.

> **Q: What if I find a bug?**
> A: Reply to your order email — 24-hour response. Bundle buyers are flagged for proactive update emails when we ship fixes to any of the 6 included sheets.
