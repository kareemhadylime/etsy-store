## 7. FAQs (10)

> **Q: How is this different from the $9 Notion budget templates I see everywhere?**
> A: Honestly: those are blank pages. A header, an empty database, maybe a single screenshot. This is a built workspace — the dashboard, rollups, linked views, and database relationships are already wired. The $20 difference is the 4–6 hours you don't have to spend building it yourself. (If you genuinely want to learn Notion by building from scratch, the $9 ones are fine. This is for people who want to use a budget today.)

> **Q: Do I need to pay for Notion?**
> A: No. This works on Notion's free tier. The features we use (databases, rollups, formulas, linked views) are all free-tier features. If you eventually upgrade to Notion Plus or use Notion AI, the template benefits without needing anything from us.

> **Q: How does delivery work?**
> A: After purchase, Etsy sends you an email with a Notion duplicate link. Click it → Notion opens the template → click "Duplicate" at the top right → choose your workspace → done. Takes 30 seconds. The 5-page Setup PDF walks through your first 5 minutes inside the template.

> **Q: Can I customize it?**
> A: Yes — fully. Add categories. Rename databases. Change tag colors. Add new pages. Delete what you don't need. Notion's databases are flexible; the template is a starting point, not a cage. The Setup PDF includes a "common customizations" page covering the 5 things people change most.

> **Q: I don't know Notion. Will I be lost?**
> A: The Setup PDF is written for someone who has a Notion account and has used databases at least once. If you've literally never opened Notion, you can absolutely still use this — read the PDF first, then duplicate. Notion's own learning curve is the actual barrier, not this template.

> **Q: Does this connect to my bank?**
> A: No. Notion can't connect to banks — that's a Plaid feature, and Notion doesn't support Plaid. This is a manual-entry budget tracker for people who'd rather see their numbers in Notion than in Mint/Copilot/YNAB. The trade-off is privacy + Notion-native vs. automatic imports.

> **Q: What's coming in Pro and AI Edition?**
> A: Pro ($49, planned) adds the Net Worth + Investment Portfolio databases — same Notion-native design, more financial coverage. AI Edition ($69, planned) adds 30+ Notion AI prompts saved as templates inside the workspace + a sidebar of ChatGPT/Claude prompts for people who use external AI. Both tiers ship after Essentials hits sustained sales — so buying Essentials is a vote for the expansion roadmap.

> **Q: Will I get the Pro and AI Editions free if I buy Essentials now?**
> A: Pro and AI will be paid upgrades when they ship. We'll offer Essentials buyers a discount equal to your Essentials purchase price (so you don't pay twice for what you already own). Watch for the email when they launch.

> **Q: Can my partner / family share this Notion workspace with me?**
> A: Yes. Notion has native sharing — invite anyone to your workspace with view, comment, or edit access. The template doesn't restrict that. Notion's own pricing limits guest collaborators on the free tier; that's a Notion rule, not ours.

> **Q: What if Notion changes their UI and breaks the template?**
> A: We ship updates when Notion makes breaking changes (rare but it happens). Updates are free for one-time buyers (Essentials is one-time). If a Notion change requires a major rebuild, we'll announce it via the email you bought through.
