## 7. FAQs (10)

> **Q: I already own the Budget Tracker. Can I get this bundle minus that one?**
> A: Not directly — Etsy's checkout doesn't support partial bundles. What we do instead: email us proof of your prior purchase (the Etsy order number is enough) and we'll send you a coupon code for the difference. Net: you don't pay twice for the same spreadsheet. We process within 24 hours.

> **Q: What's the difference between this and the AI Edition bundle ($119)?**
> A: This Pro bundle = the 5 Pro-tier spreadsheets only. The AI Edition bundle = the same 5 spreadsheets + a 28-page master AI prompt library covering 10 cross-product workflows and ~60 per-product reference prompts. If you actively use ChatGPT/Claude, the $40 gap is the AI library. If you don't, save the $40.

> **Q: Are these the same spreadsheets you sell individually?**
> A: Yes, exactly the same Pro-tier files. Sold individually they're $115 total. Bundled, $79. The discount comes from bundling and saves you a step (one purchase vs. five).

> **Q: Does this include the Wedding spreadsheet?**
> A: No — this is the Finance Bundle. The Premium Life Bundle ($99 Pro / $149 AI) adds the Wedding Budget & Planner as a 6th SKU. If you want Wedding included, look for the Life Bundle listing on our shop.

> **Q: Why isn't Investment Portfolio Tracker / Family & Education Planner / Zakat Calculator in this bundle?**
> A: Investment is niche enough that bundling pushes the price too high for the average buyer. Family & Education and Zakat are smaller-audience products sold separately. Buyers who want everything can grab the standalone listings; bundling the core 5 was a deliberate choice to keep the value clear.

> **Q: Setup wizard — what's in it and why do I need it?**
> A: The setup wizard PDF walks through which spreadsheet to open first, second, third, etc. Reason: Net Worth pulls categories from Budget and balances from Debt Payoff. Sinking Funds shares goal data with Budget. If you set them up in the wrong order, you'll fix the same thing twice. The PDF makes you set them up once.

> **Q: Can I share the bundle with my partner?**
> A: Yes. Google Sheets has built-in sharing — invite your partner with edit access to any or all of the 5 sheets. Bundle purchase covers one Etsy buyer; what you do with Google Sheets sharing inside your own household is up to you.

> **Q: What if a spreadsheet breaks or has a bug?**
> A: Reply to your order email. We answer within 24 hours and ship fixes for free. Bundle buyers are flagged in our system so we proactively email you when we ship updates to any of the 5 included sheets.

> **Q: Excel or Google Sheets?**
> A: Google Sheets in v1. Each sheet ships with the Excel export as a courtesy file inside the same bundle download — works for most cases, but some advanced Google Sheets functions (QUERY, FILTER, IMPORTRANGE) don't have 1:1 Excel equivalents. If Excel is your primary tool, look at the individual listing's FAQ for Excel-parity status.

> **Q: Refund policy — what happens if I return one of the five?**
> A: Bundles are all-or-nothing. We can't refund 20% and let you keep four sheets — Etsy doesn't support partial digital refunds. Full refund = full access revoked across all 5. If you're unsure whether to buy the bundle, start with one or two standalone listings first.
