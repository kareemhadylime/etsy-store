## 7. FAQs (10)

> **Q: Which Madhhab does this support?**
> A: All four major Sunni schools: Hanafi, Maliki, Shafi'i, Hanbali. The Madhhab Settings tab (every tier) is a one-time selector — your choice propagates across every downstream tab's ruling logic. Specifically: gold vs silver Nisab choice, debt deduction rules (Hanafi/Maliki strict vs Shafi'i/Hanbali more permissive), stocks Zakat method preference, and Hawl interpretation all adjust per Madhhab. Khums (Shia framework) is a separate v2 product.

> **Q: How does the AI cite fatwas?**
> A: Every AI prompt response (AI Edition) includes scholarly attributions in the worked examples. The Distribution Planner cites Surah At-Tawbah 9:60 for the 8 eligible categories. The Stocks Method Picker cites NZF UK guidance and AAOIFI Standard 21 §X.Y for the speculator-vs-dividend-investor logic. The Pension Analyser cites NZF Worldwide guidance for DB/DC/SIPP treatment. The AI summarizes mainstream rulings — verify your specific situation with a qualified mufti.

> **Q: Crypto Zakat — how is it calculated?**
> A: Cryptocurrency is a first-class line item (Essentials+). Per-coin tracking: units, monthly price log, exchange/wallet, cost basis. Calculation follows mainstream NZF guidance: cryptocurrencies held as investment are Zakatable at full market value once Nisab + Hawl conditions are met. The AI Crypto/DeFi Zakat prompt (AI Edition) handles edge cases: staked positions, liquidity pool participation, lending/borrowing protocols (where the underlying treatment differs by scholar).

> **Q: EOSB / Provident Fund — what's included?**
> A: Gulf-critical content (Pro+). The EOSB & Provident Fund tab supports DB (defined benefit), DC (defined contribution), and SIPP (self-invested) detection. Splits voluntary vs compulsory contributions per AAOIFI Standard 21 rules. Includes Gulf-country selectors (Saudi/UAE/Kuwait/Qatar/Bahrain/Oman). The AI Pension Analyser walks you through the accessibility test (Zakatable only when accessible without penalty per most scholars).

> **Q: I have crypto + rental property + a Sukuk allocation + my Saudi EOSB. Can this handle all that?**
> A: Yes (Pro+). The Sukuk Tracker supports all three major Sukuk structures (Ijarah / Musharakah / Murabahah) with auto-applied rulings. The Cryptocurrency tab covers BTC/ETH/altcoins with per-position tracking. Rental Property uses the annual net income method (not market value, per most contemporary scholars). EOSB has Saudi-specific support. The Family Consolidation tab combines all of this with spouse + dependent assets for per-person Nisab calculation.

> **Q: What's Qada Zakat?**
> A: Zakat for missed years. The Qada Zakat tab (Pro+) lets you track years where Nisab was reached but Zakat wasn't paid — each year as a separate row with the wealth-at-that-year + Zakat owed + paid status. The AI Qada Recovery Coach prompt (AI Edition) generates an affordable payment plan to clear missed years over multiple months. This is the #1 feature missing from free Zakat calculators.

> **Q: How do you handle the gold vs silver Nisab debate?**
> A: Both methods supported (Pro+). The Nisab Calculator tab shows current threshold under both methods side-by-side using live gold/silver spot via GOOGLEFINANCE. The Hanafi school traditionally uses silver Nisab (which is significantly lower); Maliki/Shafi'i/Hanbali typically use gold. The AI Nisab Method Picker prompt walks you through pros/cons per scholar so you can choose informed. Your Madhhab Settings drives the default.

> **Q: Zakat al-Fitr — is that the same as Zakat al-Mal?**
> A: No, separate obligation (Essentials+). Zakat al-Fitr is paid before Eid Salah on behalf of every family member, equal to ~2.5kg of staple food or its cash equivalent. The Zakat al-Fitr Calculator tab handles per-family-member rows with local-region cash-equivalent lookups. The Dashboard shows a Zakat al-Fitr alert ribbon during Ramadan + 30 days prior so you don't miss the window.

> **Q: Does it work in Excel?**
> A: No. This is Google Sheets only. The gold/silver spot prices + 9-currency FX rates require GOOGLEFINANCE, which is a Sheets-exclusive function. Excel cannot pull live spot prices. We're transparent about this — Pro/AI Edition specifically needs Sheets.

> **Q: What if I find a calculation error or need a scholar review?**
> A: Reply to your order email — 24-hour response. Bug fixes free forever. AI Edition buyers get 12 months of free template refreshes that ship before Ramadan each Hijri year (tax-deductible-receipt format for US/UK/CA buyers updates annually with current rules). If you spot a scholarly discrepancy, send your reasoning + source — we update with attribution when correcting.
