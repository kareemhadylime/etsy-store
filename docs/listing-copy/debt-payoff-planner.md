## 7. FAQs (10)

> **Q: Is this a Google Sheet or an Excel file?**
> A: Google Sheets primary; Excel courtesy export included in the same download. Some Pro/AI features (Credit Score Simulator's bureau-weighted formulas, 3-bureau matrix logic) only work cleanly in Sheets. If Excel is your primary tool, the core debt-payoff math still works; the bureau-comparison features need Sheets.

> **Q: How is this different from Tally or Credit Karma?**
> A: Three things. (1) Privacy — your debt data and credit scores stay in your Google Drive, not on a SaaS server. Credit Karma is "free" because they sell your profile to lenders; we don't have a way to sell your data because we never see it. (2) Price — $12–$29 once vs. Tally $25/month forever. Over 5 years that's $1,471+ saved. (3) Control — no auto-pay surprises, no APR adjustments without your say-so. You drive every decision.

> **Q: Does it sync with my bank or credit bureau?**
> A: No, and that's the privacy gate. You enter balances manually (or paste from your bank's statement). You log your credit score from your free annual report (or your bank's free score). The 5 minutes a week of manual entry is the trade-off for zero data exposure. Tally pulls via Plaid; we don't.

> **Q: Snowball vs. Avalanche — which method should I pick?**
> A: Avalanche saves more total interest (highest APR first = math-optimal). Snowball builds momentum faster (smallest balance first = first win in weeks). The Strategy Comparison Matrix tab shows both side-by-side with your actual debt data — debt-free date and total interest for each. Pick whichever you'll actually stick to. Switch any time; your data carries over.

> **Q: Credit Score Simulator — is it real or just educational?**
> A: It's a real model based on FICO's published weighting (35% payment history / 30% utilization / 15% length / 10% mix / 10% inquiries). The simulator uses your inputs to project a point range — not a guaranteed number, but a realistic estimate of impact. It can't predict the bureau's exact calculation (no one can — they keep that proprietary). Use it for "should I pay Card A or Card B first?" type decisions; it gives you the answer.

> **Q: Do I need ChatGPT Plus or Claude Pro for AI features?**
> A: No. The 7 AI prompts (AI Edition only) work in ChatGPT's free tier or Claude's free tier. You paste the prompt + your data into your own AI tool. Nothing in this spreadsheet sends data to any AI service — the AI part happens in your own browser, in your own account.

> **Q: How many debts can I track?**
> A: Essentials supports up to 10 debts. Pro and AI Edition support up to 20. For most buyers, 10 is plenty (mortgage + car + 2 credit cards + student loan + medical = 6). If you have more than 20, you might be a candidate for the Bundle which includes the Net Worth Tracker (which handles unlimited liabilities).

> **Q: Does it work for federal student loans / PSLF / IDR?**
> A: Yes (Pro+). The Student Loan Tab handles federal vs. private split, IDR vs. standard repayment, PSLF eligibility tracking, and an aggressive-extra-payment simulator. If you're going for PSLF (120 qualifying payments), the tab tracks your count + projected forgiveness date.

> **Q: Will my credit score actually improve?**
> A: Depends on what's bringing it down. The Debt Health Score and Credit Score Tracker tell you which of the 5 FICO factors is hurting you most. The AI Credit Score Coach (AI Edition) gives you a ranked action list with estimated point gains per action. We don't promise specific point gains (no one can promise that without lying), but we show you the levers.

> **Q: What if I find a bug or have a feature request?**
> A: Reply to your order email — 24-hour response. Bug fixes free forever. AI Edition buyers get 12 months of free template refreshes; Essentials and Pro are one-time purchases with free bug fixes only.
