- Snowball method (smallest balance first)

- Payoff timeline with month-by-month breakdown
- Total interest paid comparison (Snowball vs Avalanche)
- Minimum payment tracker
- Available in Google Sheets + Excel

### Pro — $24
- Everything in Essentials
- Custom hybrid payoff method (user-defined order)
- Balance transfer analyzer — input new APR + transfer fee %, shows true break-even month and net savings vs. current path
- Student loan tab — income-driven repayment vs. standard vs. aggressive extra payment
- Debt-free countdown timer (days remaining, visual progress bar)
- Celebration milestones (auto-generated — flags when each debt hits $0)
- Dark mode dashboard

### AI Edition — $36
- Everything in Pro
- **Payoff Strategy Optimizer** — GPT prompt asks 3 questions (need quick wins? high income? variable cash flow?) → recommends single best strategy with reasoning
- **Balance Transfer Intelligence** — AI evaluates whether to transfer, which card to target first, and warns about common traps (teaser rate expiry, over-limit fees)
- **Income Acceleration Ideas** — GPT generates 5 realistic side income ideas based on user's stated profession and available hours, with estimated monthly earning range
- **Refinancing Radar** — monthly prompt flags optimal window to refinance based on current debt balance, credit score input, and market rate input
