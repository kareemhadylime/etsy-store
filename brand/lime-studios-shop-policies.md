# Lime Studios — Shop policies (Policy settings)

**Context:** every product is an **instant digital download** (spreadsheets/planners the buyer keeps forever). Etsy treats digital items as non-returnable, so the physical "30-day simple policy" does **not** apply. Set policies that are honest about digital + buyer-friendly on support.

> These are entered manually in Shop Manager → Settings → Policy settings (the Etsy API can't set them; token is expired regardless). Exact toggles may vary slightly by Etsy version. After creating, **attach each policy to your listings** (the 13 drafts need it).

---

## 1. Returns & exchanges
**Do NOT click "Use simple policy"** (it's the physical 30-day template — buyer pays return shipping, etc.). Click **Create policy** and set returns/exchanges to **No** (digital items can't be returned). Add this as the buyer-facing note (and repeat it in each listing description + FAQ):

> Every Lime Studios product is an instant digital download you keep forever, so I'm not able to offer returns or exchanges. But I stand behind every file: if something won't open, has an error, or doesn't match the listing, message me and I'll fix or replace it quickly. Your success with the tool is the whole point.

*Alternative (only if you want a conversion boost and accept some abuse risk): offer a 14-day "file-problem" refund limited to genuine technical issues — not "changed my mind," since buyers keep the download. Ask me and I'll draft it.*

## 2. Cancellations
Set **accept cancellations: Yes**, with the instant-delivery condition. Buyer-facing note:

> I accept cancellations before delivery. Digital orders are delivered instantly, so once a file has been downloaded the sale is final. Bought by mistake and haven't downloaded yet? Message me within 24 hours and I'll cancel and refund in full.

## 3. Privacy
Paste:

> **What I collect:** only the order details Etsy shares with me — your name, email, and what you bought — used solely to deliver your order and provide support.
> **What I don't do:** I never sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing.
> **Email list (optional):** if you choose to join, I'll send occasional product updates — unsubscribe anytime with one click.
> **Your rights:** message me any time to access, correct, or delete the information I hold. I handle data in line with Etsy's Privacy & Data Policy and applicable laws, including the GDPR and CCPA.

## 4. Fixed policies
Nothing to write here. **Fixed policies** are Etsy-authored, non-editable terms you *opt into* (rather than draft). Open the tab, review the standard terms relevant to digital downloads, and accept. It's a read-and-accept that adds a layer of clarity/protection on top of the custom policies above.

---

## After setup
- Attach the Returns, Cancellations, and Privacy policies to all listings (incl. the 13 drafts).
- Keep the returns note also inside each listing's description + the shop FAQ — buyers read those before policies.
