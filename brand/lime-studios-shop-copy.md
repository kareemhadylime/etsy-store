## About your shop (story)

> Hi — I'm Karim Hady, the founder behind Lime Studios.
>
> I don't just design these planners — I live in them. My background spans electronics, computer engineering, and software, paired with business management and years of hands-on entrepreneurship — building and running several companies, from my early years in Canada to the businesses I lead today across Cairo and Dubai.
>
> That mix is the whole point. The engineering side builds systems that don't break and logic you can trust; the business side keeps the numbers honest and tied to real decisions. Every tool in this shop is where those two worlds meet — which is also why each one had to pass a single test before it earned a place here: would I trust it with my own money? If the answer was no, it never made the shelf.
>
> The idea behind all of it is simple. Managing your money shouldn't take a finance degree, a dozen disconnected apps, or a subscription that bills you forever. It should take one well-designed spreadsheet and ten honest minutes.
>
> So every planner follows the same rule: you enter your numbers in one clean Input tab, and a colourful Dashboard instantly shows you where you stand — what's coming in, what's going out, and exactly what to do next. No broken formulas. No guesswork. No fluff.
>
> Today the shop covers the whole money journey — budgeting, paying off debt, building sinking funds, tracking net worth and investments, planning for family and education, even Zakat and big life events. Each one comes in three editions: Essentials for a clean start, Pro for deeper control, and an AI Edition that reads your numbers and hands you a personalised next step.
>
> I build for real life and real currencies — whether you're in Cairo, Dubai, or anywhere in between — so the tools work the way your money actually moves.
>
> Buy once, own it forever, and update it as often as you like. If anything doesn't work the way you expect, message me — I read every note personally.
>
> Thank you for stopping by. I hope these tools give you what they gave me: a calm, clear view of your money, and the quiet confidence that comes with it.
> — Karim Hady
